What this playground is for

A testing space, not a live release

  • Check which ARASAAC symbols are returned for real-world communication needs.
  • Spot ambiguous pictures, confusing labels and missing concepts.
  • Record which BSL entries need genuine video demonstrations and review.
  • Keep all experiments separate from the main app and its Vercel build quota.

This site is static. Symbol searches call ARASAAC directly from your browser. No SignBuddy server, database, account or Vercel deployment is involved.
